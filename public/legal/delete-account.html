Delete your KTL account

In the app

  1. Open KTL and go to House.
  2. Choose Delete your account, then Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you’re signed out.

Without the app

Email CONTACT_EMAIL from the address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my KTL account”. We’ll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, name and email address.
  • Everything in “Yours”: pay, personal bills, debts, savings and your plan.
  • Your device’s push notification token.
  • Any household where you’re the only member, with everything in it.
  • In a household you share: bills and loans you paid for, your share of the others, and your activity.

What’s kept

In a household you share, the rest of its history — bills other people paid, and their own activity — stays with them. Their activity lines can still mention you by name (for example “Settled up with Alex”), because they’re part of their record. If you still owed or were owed money there, it’s written off when you leave.
